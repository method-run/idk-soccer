⚽ Grid Soccer

A dice & formation board game on a 9×18 pitch

Reds0
Turn 1/40
0Blues

How to play

Win: most goals after 50 turns (25 each).

Your turn

  1. Formation (optional, once/turn): tap a card. At the end of your turn every uncontrolled teammate drifts 1 square toward its slot (diamond markers). Slots slide sideways toward the ball.
  2. Your mover is forced: the teammate holding the ball, or your closest to it (glowing ring). Move up to SPD squares — only SPD−2 while dribbling.
  3. One ball action: pass, shoot, or steal.

Dice (all rolls are 2d6 + stat ≥ target)

  • Pass (PAS): target 6, +1 per 3 squares of distance. Miss = ball scatters 1–2 squares. Anyone standing where it lands takes it.
  • Shoot (SHO): target 8, +1 per 2 squares beyond 2, +1 for corner columns. Rolling doubles on an accurate shot = unstoppable. Otherwise the keeper secretly dives at one of the 6 goal cells: exact cell = save, right column = save on 2d6+CTL ≥ 8, wrong way = goal. Miss by 1 = loose rebound.
  • Loose ball: land on it. Free if no opponent is adjacent; otherwise opposed 2d6+CTL (tie = you).
  • Steal (CTL): stand adjacent to the carrier, roll 2d6+CTL ≥ 8 + their CTL.

🎲 Plays on paper too: a 9×18 grid, 14 coins, 2 six-sided dice, and the numbers above.
