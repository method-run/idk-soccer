⚽ Grid Soccer

A dice & formation board game on a 9×18 pitch

Draft

Set your lineup

Tap two players to swap their formation slots. Slot order runs defense → attack and applies to every formation card.

0′ 1st Half

How to play

Win: most goals after 50 turns (25 each). The clock maps turns onto 90 minutes.

Your turn

  1. Formation (optional, switch freely): tap your team panel's card, as often as you like before ending your turn. Arrows always preview every teammate's auto-move (1 square toward its slot, taken when your turn ends). Slots slide sideways toward the ball.
  2. Pick a footballer: defaults to the ball carrier (or your closest player to the ball). Tap another teammate to control them instead — until you move or act, then you're committed.
  3. Movement is a step budget (SPD squares; SPD−1 while dribbling) spent in any number of segments, before and/or after your action — zig-zag as you like. You can run through occupied squares (never stop on them); dribbling through an opponent triggers a challenge: opposed 2d6+CTL (+support, ties win). Lose by 1–2 and the ball is knocked loose; lose by 3+ and the defender steals it where they stand — your run still finishes.
  4. One ball action: tap your selected player to cycle views — action ring (with odds) → stats → minimal. Ring actions: pass, shoot, steal.

Undo/redo movement freely — but every dice roll is a checkpoint: once dice hit the table, nothing before them can be taken back.

Dice (all rolls are 2d6 + stat ≥ target)

  • Pass (PAS): target 6, +1 per 3 squares of distance. Miss = ball scatters 1–2 squares. Anyone standing where it lands takes it.
  • Shoot (SHO): target 8, +1 per 2 squares beyond 2, +1 for corner cells. Doubles on an accurate shot = unstoppable. Otherwise the keeper secretly dives at one of the 6 goal cells: exact cell = save, right row = save on 2d6+CTL ≥ 8, wrong way = goal. Miss by 1 = loose rebound.
  • Loose ball: land on it. Free if no opponent is adjacent; otherwise opposed 2d6+CTL (tie = you).
  • Steal (CTL): stand adjacent to the carrier, roll 2d6+CTL ≥ 8 + their CTL.
  • Outnumbering: on pickups and steals, add +1 per extra teammate adjacent to the contest (beyond the opponents there), capped at ±2. A 2-on-1 is a real advantage.

🎲 Plays on paper too: a 9×18 grid, 14 coins, 2 six-sided dice, and the numbers above.
